REYER is choreographer, curator, and producer working across multidisciplinary performance, nightlife, and embodied research, whose experimental practice mobilises whoreography, the somatics of substance, and club-born socialities to construct embodied archives and counter-narratives. They weave lived experience, mythology, and digital objects into collective imaginaries that challenge dominant pulls +  themselves on stage, shaping new visibilities and making spaces and ways for ‘’Hardcore Softies’’ moving through an overstimulated world, REYER does this frequently by blurring the audiences contracts between passive/active performers, cultivating uncanny and transmissive works every showing. 

Their work has been presented across European artistic and cultural contexts, including Oude Kerk, FOAM, Frascati, and VEEM House in Amsterdam, as well as NEWFEARS Gallery in Berlin, Grand Carmes Brussels and Ornamenta Pforzheim. Alongside their gallery and institutional practice, their nightlife-oriented works have been situated within club and party contexts including Raum, Curch, Pornceptual, Horst Club Brussels, and many more engaging with the social, spatial, and camp-oriented performative dimensions of nightlife culture.

REYER graduated from the SNDO School for New Dance Development in Amsterdam in 2026, following several years of self-directed practice as a visual artist. Their multidisciplinary practice operates across visual art, performance, and nightlife, investigating forms of collective experience, embodiment, and cultural production
