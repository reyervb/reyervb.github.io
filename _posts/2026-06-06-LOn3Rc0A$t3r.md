‘L0N3rc0a$T3r live’ stages a fictional mythology around Viagra Falls, a creature formed from accumulated lived experience and the residue of missed possibilities. Here, what could not happen is not treated as absence, but transmitted into materials: sound, gesture, language, objects, pharmacological fiction, rhythm, and the unstable architecture of the clubborns.

Viagra Falls presents itself as the chosen channel of the Oracle of Sound, conducting a ceremony in which personal history is continuously transformed into collective experience. The audience is invited to participate through a placebo pill, a seemingly harmless proposition that materialises a larger question: what does it mean to enter a situation when participation itself produces consequence?

The work moves through states of seduction, belief, intimacy, suspicion, grief, and excess. As the performer consumes too many pills, the connection to the Oracle deteriorates. What follows is an uncontrolled choreography of rupture: trust repeatedly becomes accusation, vulnerability becomes blame, and the audience is implicated in the performer’s failed transmission.

The club gradually shifts from being a spatial container into a corporeal condition. Performers and audience become a shared, unstable body, one that carries the afterlife of choices not made, encounters missed, connections lost, and futures that never materialised. L0N3rc0a$T3r live is therefore a ceremony of transmission: an attempt to give material form to what remains when an experience has already become impossible.
